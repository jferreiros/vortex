# Research 04 — Voice-agent industry, Sep 2025 to Sep 2026

Scope: launches and claims relevant to a clinic scheduling voice agent on Twilio (8 kHz mu-law), built with Pipecat 1.11, Soniox STT, Google/ElevenLabs TTS, OpenAI-compatible LLM.
Research date: 2026-09-19. Claims come from vendor posts unless marked. UNVERIFIED marks claims I could only see in secondary sources.

## Summary

- Cascaded STT -> LLM -> TTS stays the industry recommendation for tool-heavy, regulated tasks like booking. LiveKit (Mar 2026), Coval (Jan 2026) and Deepgram say so in writing.
- Speech-to-speech models improved fast (gpt-realtime Aug 2025, GPT-Realtime-2 May 2026, GPT-Live-1 Sep 2026, Gemini 3.8 Live Sep 2026). Their agentic task scores stay low: best model 52.1% on tau-Voice (Artificial Analysis, Jun 2026).
- Turn detection became a model, not a silence timer. Smart Turn v3.1 (Dec 2025), Deepgram Flux (Oct 2025), LiveKit Turn Detector v1 (Jun 2026) and Soniox semantic endpointing (Feb 2026) all shipped.
- Alphanumeric and named-entity accuracy is the top failure mode. ServiceNow EVA (Mar 2026) found entity transcription errors cascade into authentication failures. OpenAI (Jul 2026), Google (Sep 2026) and Speechmatics (Aug 2026) each shipped fixes.
- Evals moved into the frameworks. Pipecat ships scripted and simulated scenarios with an LLM judge. Vapi (Dec 2025) and ElevenLabs (2026) ship simulations with tool mocks.
- Guardrail guidance converged on layers: untrusted-content wrapping, backend-enforced tool permissions, output filters, post-call redaction. ElevenLabs (Aug 2025) and Hamming (Jun 2026) publish the checklists.
- Healthcare voice agents raised big rounds on scheduling: Assort Health $120M at $1.2B (Jun 2026), Hyro $45M (Oct 2025), Bland $50M Series C (Jun 2026). Published resolution rates run 48% to 97%.
- Latency targets: 500 to 800 ms voice-to-voice is the marketed goal. Daily's primer (Jun 2026) calls 1,500 ms "an important target" and shows a 1,293 ms budget.

## Speech-to-speech vs cascaded

**What moved.**
- OpenAI shipped four S2S generations in 13 months: gpt-realtime (Aug 2025), GPT-Realtime-2 (May 2026), 2.1 (Jul 2026), GPT-Live-1 (Sep 2026). GPT-Live-1 splits the job: a full-duplex voice layer delegates reasoning and tools to a backend model.
- Google matched with Gemini 3.8 Live and "Extended Thinking" (Sep 2026). Amazon shipped Nova 2 Sonic with async tool calling (Dec 2025).
- Kyutai keeps Moshi for latency but recommends Unmute (cascaded) for function calling and reasoning.

**Latency numbers.**
- Coval: S2S 200-300 ms vs ~2,000 ms for a naive cascade. LiveKit: a streaming cascade reaches 400-800 ms.
- ElevenLabs measured budget: P50 ~680 ms, P95 ~1,560 ms. Daily's primer: ~1,293 ms typical, 500 ms achievable with co-location.

**Tool-calling reliability of S2S.**
- Artificial Analysis (Jun 2026): agentic tau-Voice is "the hardest dimension by a wide margin"; every S2S model scores below 53%.
- tau-Voice paper (Mar 2026): voice agents keep 30-45% of text-agent capability; noise and accents cut success to 26-38%.
- OpenAI community reports on gpt-realtime-2.1 mention function-calling problems in structured workflows (anecdotal).
- Deepgram: "support isn't the same as reliability."

**Recommendation for booking.**
- LiveKit (Mar 2026): "Text-based LLM tool calling is more mature and predictable than audio-native alternatives."
- Coval (Jan 2026): healthcare should stay cascaded; there is no text intermediary for compliance checks.
- Our pipeline (Soniox -> text LLM -> TTS) matches the 2026 default. Say this to the jury with the tau-Voice number.

## Techniques to cite

1. **Three-layer turn detection.** VAD with a 200 ms trigger, a native-audio turn model (Smart Turn), and an LLM single-token tag (complete / short pause / long pause). Source: Daily primer, Jun 2026, https://voiceaiandvoiceagents.com/. Numbers: Smart Turn v3.1 Spanish 91.0%, https://www.daily.co/blog/improved-accuracy-in-smart-turn-v3-1/.
2. **Semantic endpointing in STT.** Soniox v4 uses context and rhythm, with `max_endpoint_delay_ms`. https://soniox.com/blog/2026-02-05-soniox-v4-real-time. Deepgram Flux eager EOT: 150-250 ms earlier at +50-70% LLM calls. https://deepgram.com/learn/introducing-flux-conversational-speech-recognition.
3. **Preemptive (speculative) LLM generation.** LiveKit runs the LLM before end-of-turn is confirmed, on by default; `preemptive_tts` extends it to TTS. https://docs.livekit.io/agents/build/audio/. ElevenLabs: feed stable partials, revise on final. https://elevenlabs.io/blog/voice-agent-latency-optimization.
4. **Sentence-level TTS chunking.** Stream LLM tokens into TTS at sentence or clause boundaries. ElevenLabs (Jun 2026) and LiveKit (Mar 2026) both describe it. Pipecat budgets 20 ms for sentence aggregation.
5. **Codec matching for telephony.** Request `ulaw_8000` from TTS to avoid transcoding; Scribe v2 Realtime accepts mu-law input. https://elevenlabs.io/blog/voice-agent-latency-optimization, https://elevenlabs.io/blog/introducing-scribe-v2-realtime.
6. **Thinking and ambient sounds.** LiveKit ships "ambient sounds, thinking sounds, and on-demand audio playback" for tool-call gaps. https://docs.livekit.io/agents/build/audio/ (detail page returned 404 on fetch).
7. **Async guardrail inference.** Run moderation or policy checks as background jobs so the conversation continues. Daily primer, section on async inference.
8. **Function-call context hygiene.** Always store request/response pairs; mark cancelled calls as `CANCELLED`; more than five tools degrades accuracy, so build evals. Daily primer.
9. **Structured outputs for tools.** Pipecat 1.0 uses provider-agnostic `FunctionSchema` / `ToolsSchema`. https://docs.pipecat.ai/pipecat/migration/migration-1.0. Vendors now ship JSON-Schema tool registration in STT streams too (AssemblyAI Voice Agent API).
10. **Post-call judge ("arbiter").** Hamming rubric: deterministic checks first, then an LLM grader with evidence spans, confidence, `critical_failure`, `needs_human_review`. https://hamming.ai/resources/llm-grader-voice-agent-call-scoring-rubric. Pattern with two judges, one blind to the task: https://www.faridfadaie.com/2026/06/17/llm-as-a-judge-voice-agents/ (independent blog). Cartesia Line offers custom LLM-as-judge metrics. https://www.cartesia.ai/blog/introducing-line-for-voice-agents.
11. **Dual verification of extracted fields.** No vendor post names it. Closest sources: Hamming's "deterministic first" table, and EVA's finding that entity errors cascade into auth failures. Present it as our design, cite those two.
12. **Semantic caching of TTS.** No primary vendor source found in the window. Treat as UNVERIFIED industry practice; describe it as our optimization.
13. **Prompt-injection defenses for phone.** Six layers: spoken input, transcript normalization, context boundaries, tool permissions, output policy, evidence logging. Backend validates identity, authorization, idempotency and confirmations, not the LLM. https://hamming.ai/resources/voice-agent-prompt-injection-testing-guide. ElevenLabs: manipulation guardrails can end the call; post-call redaction bleeps audio. https://elevenlabs.io/blog/safety-framework-for-ai-voice-agents.
14. **PII redaction.** AssemblyAI redacts PII in text and audio; ElevenLabs redacts history; Prosper claims zero data retention. Sources above.
15. **Evals as code.** Pipecat Evals: scripted and simulated scenarios, `text_contains`, `eval:` judge, tool-call expectations, absence checks, `within_ms` latency gates, interruption tests. https://docs.pipecat.ai/pipecat/fundamentals/evaluations/overview. Vapi Evals (Dec 2025) and ElevenLabs simulations with `tool_mock_overrides` (2026 changelog) do the same. https://elevenlabs.io/docs/changelog.
16. **Eval platforms.** Coval, Hamming (4M+ calls tested), Cekura, Roark (10M minutes), Bluejay, Braintrust, Cyara, Evalion, SuperBryn, TestZeus, Tuner. https://www.speechmatics.com/company/articles-and-news/de-risk-your-voice-agent-11-best-voice-agent-testing-platforms. Open source: Pipecat Evals, ServiceNow EVA https://github.com/ServiceNow/eva, voice-eval https://github.com/rand0wn/voice-eval, tau-Voice.
17. **Noise robustness.** tau-Voice shows a 5-13 point drop with noise and accents. Scribe v2 Realtime claims best results on 500 noisy samples. Cite both when you show the 5 dB SNR problem.

## Jury-ready numbers

- Voice-to-voice: 1,500 ms target, 1,293 ms typical, 500 ms achievable (Daily primer, Jun 2026).
- ElevenLabs budget: P50 680 ms, P95 1,560 ms; LLM TTFT is the largest slice (250/600 ms).
- Streaming cascade 400-800 ms vs 1,000-2,000 ms naive (LiveKit, Mar 2026). Vapi markets sub-500 ms average.
- S2S agentic ceiling: 52.1% on tau-Voice; index leader GPT-Realtime-2 at 77.2% (Artificial Analysis, Jun 2026).
- Voice vs text: 31-51% vs 85% task success; 26-38% with noise and accents (tau-Voice, Mar 2026).
- Multi-turn spoken dialogue: best model 54.65% (Audio MultiChallenge, Dec 2025).
- Turn detection: LiveKit v1 9.9% false cutoffs at 300 ms; Soniox 5.5% at 600 ms (LiveKit, Jun 2026). Smart Turn v3.1 Spanish 91.0%.
- Streaming STT: AssemblyAI 8.14% WER English, sub-200 ms (Apr 2026). Gemini 3.5 Transcribe 4.0% streaming WER (Sep 2026).
- S2S adoption: under 15% of production agents in H1 2026 (Coval).
- Healthcare market: $876M in 2026 to $3.18B by 2030, 37.8% CAGR (Grand View via CloudTalk). UNVERIFIED primary.
- No-shows: automated calls cut no-show rate 11.3% to 9.6% across 244,000 patients (NEJM Catalyst 2025, via CloudTalk). UNVERIFIED primary.
- Resolution rates: Assort 97%, Prosper ~50%, Hello Patient 48% (billing) and 30% (urgent care).
- Scale: Bland 3.5M conversations/week; Hyro 30M patients; Hamming 10K+ agents and 10M+ minutes tested; Deepgram 400+ enterprise customers.
- Prices: GPT-Realtime-2 $32/$64 per 1M audio tokens; GPT-Live-1 $0.05/min; Gemini 3.8 Live $0.005 in, $0.018 out per min; AssemblyAI Voice Agent API $4.50/hr.
